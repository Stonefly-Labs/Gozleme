# Release boundary: MVP versus later

**MVP:** runnable Compose/Helm-capable app, multi-org local/OIDC identities, public/private RLS and overlays, typed immutable catalog publishing, filesystem/Azure Blob/S3 artifact providers, lexical and optional pgvector hybrid semantic search, approvals and typed policies, evidence/facts/epochs, signed origin import, verifiable snapshot and incremental sync, scoped private grants and withdrawals, revocation/freshness and quarantine, deterministic bounded resolution/lockfiles, sandbox testing playground, audit and operational validation. Every feature starts with a secure minimal workflow and cumulative demo.

**Post-MVP unless security-profile review determines needed sooner:** sophisticated cross-registry publishing-authority delegation and continuity, pre-established orphan crypto recovery ceremonies, independent transparency witness/gossip, reusable federated policy packs, rich policy simulation, elaborate delegated admin recovery and advanced governance convenience tooling, optional external/vector-search engines beyond pgvector. Deferrals do not permit falsely claiming full security guarantees; unknown or unsupported trust chains must be rejected.
